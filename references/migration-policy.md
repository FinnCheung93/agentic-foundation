# Migration Policy

Upgrade is incremental alignment, not re-initialization. Inspect the active entrance, project
map, current state, existing foundation location, and only related module or archive paths.
Preserve project-specific additions, history, and links.

- Do not overwrite whole files, silently delete, or automatically move material.
- A legacy `.foundation/` may remain in place. Suggest a move to `docs/foundation/` only when
  it would reduce active-entry confusion, and wait for confirmation.
- Treat old `SUGGESTIONS.md` or `CHECKS.md` as legacy artifacts. Keep their uncertain content
  where it is; do not bulk-promote it into principles or decision records.
- If old material pollutes an active map or fractures a needed history, propose an archive or
  migration with preserved paths, affected entrances, and a rollback approach.
- After a confirmed migration, establish one active authority for each concern and update live
  links. Historical records remain historical; new indexes or records describe supersession.

Before reporting completion, summarize each default core file as updated, retained with reason,
or awaiting a user decision.
