# Record Routing

Choose the smallest existing artifact that preserves what future work actually needs.

| Need | Preferred home |
|---|---|
| Current progress, next move, active blocker, shared attention | `STATE.md` |
| Current long-lived rule, stable preference, boundary | `PRINCIPLES.md` |
| Detailed timeline, work performed, verdict, evidence or artifact link | `LOG.md` |
| Confirmed important tradeoff whose rationale will matter later | Decision Record, if enabled |
| Historical material that should leave the active map | Archive index, if enabled |
| Project overview or active entry point | Root `README.md` |

Keep detail in the appropriate source. Other files receive only the changed summary or pointer
their purpose needs. History may grow; routine context should track present needs.

Revise `STATE.md` for the next action: replace obsolete information and remove history-only
detail once it is preserved in its source or `LOG.md`. Keep concise completed outcomes that
still matter, effective authorization and constraints, and ongoing parallel work. `LOG.md`
can retain detailed chronology. Current conclusions need enough evidence and scope to recheck
when their basis changes.

When a meaningful failure has escaped an existing defense, retain the smallest useful chain:
what failed, the cause or current hypothesis, the repair, the proportionate prevention or
recheck, and where the evidence lives. Do not turn ordinary friction into a permanent process.

## File Maintenance

When creating or revising material, or closing a stage of work, maintain the organization of
the affected files. Split, merge, name, or archive them when it improves retrieval or upkeep,
following the project's conventions. Update affected active links and indexes, and verify
that content remains accessible. Preserve historical content and explain its new location in
the index. Judge organization by usefulness, not uniform file sizes or folder depth.

Handle reversible routine organization within the authorized scope directly. Explain the
impact and recovery approach before overwrites, irrecoverable deletion, high-risk migration,
or expansion of scope, and obtain confirmation. New modules follow the add-on rules.
